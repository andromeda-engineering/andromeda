# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion constructive and
on-topic. Harassment, discrimination, and abusive behavior are not tolerated.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer directly. The
maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may take any action
deemed appropriate.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
